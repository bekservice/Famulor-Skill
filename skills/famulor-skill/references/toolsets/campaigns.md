# Campaigns toolset

Campaigns, Audience contacts, leads, segments, consent records, and suppression. Connect the `settings` group as well when you need workspace consent mode or outbound limits. Connect only this group with `https://app.famulor.io/mcp?toolsets=campaigns`.

This 2026-11-12 snapshot covers all 43 tools assigned to `campaigns` in the canonical 541-tool registry. The live MCP `tools/list` response is authoritative for arguments, current availability, annotations, and plan or role gating. Never invent fields from this catalog.

Toolsets limit discovery context; they do not grant access. OAuth/API scopes and workspace roles are enforced separately. The Effect column reflects MCP risk annotations: `Delete/destructive` also covers overwrites or changes that can remove data or safeguards.

**Caution:** Campaign starts and contact changes affect real recipients. Erasing customer memory is permanent; removing suppression can restore contactability. Verify lawful consent and the exact target before these actions.

| Tool | Effect | Accepted scope | Execution | Purpose snapshot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `add_lead` | Write/action | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Add a single lead (contact) to a campaign with an optional email address. The phone number is normalized to E.164 and the email is validated and normalized. Existing contacts with the same phone or email are rejected instead of silently merged. Numbers on the tenant's do-not-call list are stored with status 'do_not_call' and are never dialed. custom_fields are available to the assistant during the call. |
| `add_leads` | Write/action | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Add up to 1000 leads (contacts) to a campaign in one request, each with an optional email address. Phone numbers are normalized to E.164 and emails are validated and normalized. Existing contacts with the same phone or email are rejected instead of silently merged. Numbers on the tenant's do-not-call list are stored with status 'do_not_call' and are never dialed. Returns the created leads plus counts of DNC-flagged and invalid rows. |
| `add_suppression_entry` | Write/action | `suppression:write or campaigns:write` | Immediate | Add a contact identified by contact_id, phone, or email to the suppression list. direction outbound (default) records a marketing opt-out: the contact is never called, texted, or emailed. direction inbound blocks incoming contact: calls from the phone number are hung up immediately and SMS, WhatsApp, other messengers, and email from that phone or email get no AI reply and no reply charge (messages stay in History; incoming SMS/RCS on purchased numbers still cost the normal receive fee). both does both. If the contact already has an entry with the other direction, it is upgraded to both. The current workspace consent mode determines whether outbound blocking covers every linked channel or only the supplied channel; inbound blocking always covers every channel of that phone or email. |
| `assign_leads_to_campaign` | Write/action | `leads:write or campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Assign leads to a campaign — either from a saved segment OR an explicit list of lead IDs (provide exactly one of the two). Unassigned contacts are moved in directly; contacts already in this campaign or currently queued/calling are skipped. A contact assigned to another campaign causes a conflict before any assignments: remove it from that campaign first. Contacts are never duplicated. Optional custom_fields are merged onto each assigned lead (existing keys kept unless overwritten). Returns assigned/skipped counts and copied=0 for compatibility. |
| `cancel_campaign_schedule` | Delete/destructive | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Cancel the scheduled start of a campaign: it returns to 'draft', and its schedule and launch attestation are cleared, so the next launch attests again. |
| `create_audience_contact` | Write/action | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Create an unassigned Audience contact, or upsert/open an existing contact with the same phone or email. Does not silently merge distinct contacts. User-defined tags are normalized and merged with existing tags. |
| `create_campaign` | Write/action | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Create a Call, WhatsApp template, WhatsApp Call (Beta), or SMS campaign. Omit channel for a backward-compatible phone_call campaign. Phone campaigns may carry a sequence (a multi-step plan of calls and SMS, WhatsApp or email messages); only a campaign with a sequence can also carry a number pool (local presence), contact-time-zone calling windows, holidays and double dial (without a sequence they are rejected and the classic retry policy applies). Add leads with add_leads, then start with start_campaign (classic) or launch_campaign (sequence, or to schedule). |
| `create_segment` | Write/action | `segments:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Save a lead filter as a reusable segment. Its matches are resolved live (dynamic, not a snapshot) — use the segment_id with assign_leads_to_campaign to assign whoever currently matches. |
| `delete_audience_contacts` | Delete/destructive | `leads:write` | Immediate | Permanently delete up to 100 explicitly selected Audience contacts. Confirm the exact workspace and contact IDs with the user before invoking. Contacts with active campaign attempts are kept. Requires a user-owned credential from a workspace owner or admin. |
| `delete_campaign` | Delete/destructive | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Delete a campaign. Its leads are kept (detached from the campaign). |
| `delete_lead` | Delete/destructive | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Backward-compatible alias for remove_lead_from_campaign. The contact is retained in Audience and deleted=false is returned. |
| `delete_segment` | Delete/destructive | `segments:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Delete a segment. The leads it currently matches are not affected. |
| `duplicate_campaign` | Write/action | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Create a draft copy of a campaign with all its settings (sequence, number pool, schedule rules, channel configuration) but no contacts, no schedule and no launch attestation. Use it to change a sequence of a completed campaign, or to try a different sequence while keeping the original. Counts against the plan's campaign limit. |
| `erase_customer_memory` | Delete/destructive | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Permanently erase all stored summaries, preferences, and remembered variables for one Audience contact. A content-free deletion audit remains; the contact and exact channel identities remain. |
| `get_campaign` | Read-only | `campaigns:read or calls:read` | Immediate | Fetch one campaign including channel settings, schedule, follow-up, and total lead count. sequence_hash is the read-only change token of its sequence (null without one): pass it unchanged to launch_campaign. |
| `get_campaign_analytics` | Read-only | `campaigns:read or calls:read` | Immediate | Per-contact funnel of one campaign (get_campaign_stats counts leads and calls instead): dialed, connected and converted contacts with the conversion rate, the messages sent by message steps, the outcome flow of the sequence as a Sankey, activity per day, and the caller numbers used. Optional time range (at most 366 days) and caller-number filter. |
| `get_campaign_stats` | Read-only | `campaigns:read or calls:read` | Immediate | Progress aggregates for leads and deliveries, plus call count and voice duration for voice campaigns. A sequence campaign that was started also returns sequence_step_contacts: the unfinished contacts (pending, queued, calling) on each step id; a step with contacts must stay in the sequence when a paused campaign is edited. |
| `get_customer_memory` | Read-only | `leads:read or calls:read` | Immediate | Get one Audience contact's workspace and assistant memories, exact channel identities, and recent memory audit events. |
| `get_lead` | Read-only | `leads:read or calls:read` | Immediate | Get one campaign lead with editable contact fields, user-managed custom fields, and a separate read-only system_variables object. |
| `launch_campaign` | Write/action | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Launch a draft or scheduled campaign: confirm compliance and either start dialing now or schedule the start. Real contacts are called as soon as the campaign runs and the outreach window is open. Runs the same checks as start_campaign (assistant, leads, channel resources, plan limit, number pool). compliance_attestation must be true. Fails with code attestation_required without it. Pass the sequence_hash that get_campaign returned, unchanged, to make sure the sequence has not changed since the user reviewed it: a mismatch fails with code sequence_changed. |
| `list_audience_contact_channels` | Read-only | `leads:read or calls:read` | Immediate | List one Audience contact's manually manageable channel profiles. Microsoft Teams profiles expose only the current user-facing connection name; reconnect copies of the same Microsoft identity are normalized and internal account or routing identifiers are never returned. |
| `list_audience_contacts` | Read-only | `leads:read or calls:read` | Immediate | List workspace contacts across all campaigns with dynamic Call QA averages. QA scores come from the per-call History scorecard; unscored calls are excluded. |
| `list_campaign_audience_products` | Read-only | `campaigns:read` | Immediate | Read live active product choices for an eligible native Shopify audience connection. No catalog import. |
| `list_campaign_audience_segments` | Read-only | `campaigns:read` | Immediate | Read current source-owned segment choices for an eligible workspace connection. |
| `list_campaign_deliveries` | Read-only | `campaigns:read or calls:read` | Immediate | List primary and follow-up delivery attempts with customer-visible status, timestamps and errors. |
| `list_campaign_templates` | Read-only | `campaigns:read` | Immediate | List the ready-made campaign sequences of the template gallery (call steps plus SMS & RCS, WhatsApp template and email steps with outcomes, waits and texts), featured first. Each item has id, category, category_label, name, description, featured, channels, step_count, needs_sender and the full sequence in the requested language. To use one, pass its sequence as `sequence` for a new campaign or to update_campaign. When needs_sender is true, first set from_number_id (list_phone_numbers) or rcs_sender_id (list_sms_rcs_senders) on each sms step, and connector_id plus template_id (list_messaging_connectors, list_whatsapp_templates) on each whatsapp_template step; the empty values are rejected. Call steps use the campaign assistant; email steps send from its verified address. |
| `list_campaigns` | Read-only | `campaigns:read or calls:read` | Immediate | List multi-channel outreach campaigns, including primary channel, schedule, assistant/sender settings, status, and the read-only sequence_hash of each campaign's sequence (null without one). |
| `list_customer_memories` | Read-only | `leads:read or calls:read` | Immediate | List one consent- and retention-aware customer-memory entry per contact with exact channel identities. Private assistant content is never exposed in the list. |
| `list_leads` | Read-only | `leads:read or calls:read` | Immediate | List the leads (contacts) of a campaign. Optionally filter by lead status (pending, queued, calling, completed, failed, no_answer, busy, do_not_call). |
| `list_segments` | Read-only | `segments:read or calls:read` | Immediate | List the workspace's segments — saved, dynamic lead filters used for audience search and campaign lead assignment (see assign_leads_to_campaign). |
| `list_suppression_entries` | Read-only | `suppression:read or campaigns:read` | Immediate | List active suppressions across voice, SMS, email, and supported messaging channels. Each result states the contact identity, scope, direction (outbound = never contacted, inbound = incoming calls and messages get no answer, both), originating channel, and consent mode captured at opt-out. |
| `merge_audience_contacts` | Write/action | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Explicitly merge one Audience contact into another. The target contact keeps its id; phone, email, channel identities, calls, and conversations move over. The source contact is removed. Never merges automatically — the caller must choose both contacts. |
| `pause_campaign` | Write/action | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Pause a running campaign: status becomes 'paused', calls already in progress finish normally and no new contact is dialed. Continue with resume_campaign. |
| `remove_lead_from_campaign` | Delete/destructive | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Remove a lead from campaign execution without deleting the Audience contact, calls, or campaign history. |
| `remove_suppression_entry` | Delete/destructive | `suppression:write or campaigns:write` | Immediate | Restore consent for one active suppression by entry ID, E.164 phone number, or email address. This revokes the whole active suppression in every direction (outbound and inbound) and appends an opt-in audit event; it does not erase consent history. |
| `replace_audience_contact_channels` | Delete/destructive | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Replace one Audience contact's manually managed channel profiles. For Microsoft Teams, set connection_name to an active connection's visible name; it may be omitted only when exactly one Teams identity connection is active. Reconnect copies of the same Microsoft identity are normalized to the current connection name. Identity collisions are rejected and never merge contacts. |
| `resume_campaign` | Write/action | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Resume a paused campaign: it dials again right away within its outreach windows. Runs the same checks as start_campaign; a campaign that ran before keeps its launch attestation until its sequence is changed: then the resume needs compliance_attestation: true, otherwise it fails with code launch_required. |
| `start_campaign` | Write/action | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Start or resume immediately and clear any schedule. Channel resources, outreach windows, suppression, concurrency, and credits are enforced before dispatch. A campaign with a sequence that was never launched, or whose sequence was changed while it was paused, starts or resumes only with compliance_attestation: true, otherwise it fails with code launch_required; launch_campaign is the dedicated tool for a draft or scheduled campaign. |
| `stop_campaign` | Write/action | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Stop (pause) a running campaign: sets status to 'paused'. Calls already in progress finish normally; the dialer just stops dialing new leads. Resume with resume_campaign or start_campaign. |
| `update_campaign` | Write/action | `campaigns:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Update campaign identity, channel configuration, schedule, voice settings, follow-up, sequence, number pool, contact time zone mode, holidays or double dial (the last four need a sequence; removing the sequence resets them). Channel/sender/template are immutable while running. The sequence changes while the campaign is a draft or paused, also after it was started; a running campaign fails with 409 "Pause the campaign to edit the sequence.", a scheduled one must cancel its schedule, a completed one is duplicated (duplicate_campaign). A started campaign keeps every contact that is not finished on its step: the new sequence must still contain each such step with the same step type, otherwise 409 "Step <name> still has <n> contacts. Keep the step, or wait until they have moved on." (get_campaign_stats shows sequence_step_contacts). A step added to a started campaign, a kept step id that changes its step type and an outcome added to a step need an id that history never used there, else 409. A wait or delay that is already running keeps its end time; the new value applies from the next send or attempt. The number pool mode is fixed once the campaign started. Changing a sequence clears the launch confirmation: launch a draft again, resume a paused campaign with compliance_attestation (resume_campaign or start_campaign). |
| `update_customer_memory` | Write/action | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Revision-protected update of a contact's explicit memory consent or shared summary/preferences. Read the current revision first. Consent and content updates are separate operations; identity links cannot be edited. |
| `update_lead` | Write/action | `leads:write or calls:write` | Immediate | Update a campaign lead's name, phone, email, tags, or user-managed custom fields. System variables and channel identities are read-only. |
| `update_suppression_entry` | Write/action | `suppression:write or campaigns:write` | Immediate | Change the direction and/or reason of one active suppression entry by its ID (from list_suppression_entries). A direction change is recorded in the consent history. inbound and both need an entry with a phone or email. reason null or empty clears it. |
